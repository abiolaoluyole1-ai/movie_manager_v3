Minimum movie duration

Maintain target automatically

If you reject a movie, discovery can find a replacement.

Count only downloadable movies toward target

When on, Target counts movies whose source is Ready or already Downloaded, not just Accepted. Discovery will keep scanning past the target's accepted count until enough movies are actually downloadable.
